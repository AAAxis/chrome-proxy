Privacy Policy

Data Collection

This extension collects minimal data necessary for its operation:

  • Client ID for device identification
  • Proxy connection status
  • Extension settings and preferences

Data Usage

Your data is used to:

  • Maintain proxy connections
  • Provide service status updates
  • Store your preferences locally

Data Storage

All data is stored locally on your device using Chrome's storage API. No personal information is transmitted to external servers except for proxy routing.

Third-Party Services

This extension may route traffic through third-party proxy servers. Please review their privacy policies separately.

Your Rights

You can:

  • Disable the extension at any time
  • Clear stored data through Chrome settings
  • Contact us with privacy concerns
